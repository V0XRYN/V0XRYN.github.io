Cybersecurity Lab Setup

November 8, 2026 · 7 min read

A practical look at building a controlled environment for Red Team learning and tool experimentation.

A security lab is a controlled environment where you can experiment with tools, techniques, and procedures without affecting production systems or violating laws.

All work in this lab is performed on systems I own or have explicit permission to test. Nothing here is intended for unauthorized use.

Environment

Virtual machines provide isolation. A hypervisor runs guest systems — one as the attacker workstation, others as vulnerable targets.

Tooling

Tool selection is purpose-driven. Scanners, analyzers, and exploitation frameworks are used only within lab boundaries.

Methodology

Reconnaissance, scanning, enumeration, and post-exploitation are practiced in sequence. Each step is documented for learning and review.

The goal is understanding how systems can be compromised — so they can be better defended.
